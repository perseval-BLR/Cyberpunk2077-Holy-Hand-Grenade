**This work is based on "Holy Hand Grenade"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/holy-hand-grenade-15de22a4bdeb486f9d6e4641404aed8a) by CheckMatefluff (BurnBomber
on Sketchfab) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).**

**Changes to the model (CC BY 4.0 requires indicating them):** converted from glTF to a Cyberpunk 2077 mesh; reduced
from ~196 000 to 20 000 triangles; turned, scaled to 0.85 (13.7 cm) and put onto the grip of the game's throwing
knife in place of its blade; textures resized from 4096 to 2048 px, metal/roughness channels separated.

**The sound:** converted from MP3 to a 48 kHz WAV, otherwise unchanged.
