# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-11
First public release. Tested with Cyberpunk 2077 2.31.
- Holy Hand Grenade item (throwing knife base) with its own model, name, description (en, ru) and inventory icon.
- Thrown like a knife, flies with its own ballistics: bounces, rolls, knocks back off people.
- Three-second fuse with an on-screen count and the "Hallelujah" choir (Audioware, optional).
- A 19 m blast: game explosion damage, deaths within 9 m, people thrown as ragdolls, cars set off and tossed one
  after another (at most 6 per blast).
- Short cooldown (about a second).
- Keyboard binding in the Cyber Engine Tweaks overlay to get the grenade again.
